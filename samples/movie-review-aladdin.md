# Aladdin — Movie Review

*A review on the movie Aladdin for a Children's magazine*

The story of Aladdin from the Arabian Nights has always been a fascinating one, catching the attention of 6 to 60 year olds alike. Yes, for many years the cartoon versions of Aladdin have always been one of the favorite movies for not only kids but grownups as well.

The recent release from the Disney World, directed by Guy Ritchie, is a masterpiece, keeping you in rapt attention for the whole time. We watched the 3D version of this movie as a family at IMAX Luxe Cinemas in Chennai. It has been a thrilling experience for us all. The story was woven in such a way that there is no room for boredom. The movie is a full package of entertainment which includes comedy, action, musical, and love. Though it is a well-known fairy tale, the director presented the story in a way that kept the audience at the edge of their seats.

It is very obvious that the movie had an efficient script writer, as most of the dialogues are amusing, leaving the crowd in laughter. There was not one sentence that we felt was irrelevant or out of place.

The cinematography was full of radiant colors and was a visual treat for kids especially. The visual effects were so close to reality that watching this movie in 3D makes you feel that you are inside the story. There is a saying that goes, "A movie without proper sound is not a movie at all." The background score and the sound effects are much enhanced with the modern technology of Dolby Atmos. The realistic sound effects, like the roar of a tiger, will make you shudder in a fun-filled fear. Aladdin, being a musical movie, the songs and the choreography etch a mark in your memory that even long after the movie is over you will be left humming the songs and tapping to the groovy music.

Having a star cast like Will Smith to play the magical genie is like a cherry on an ice cream. The energy and the charm that he carried in that character are highly commendable. Mena Massoud, who played the role of Aladdin, was the right choice, displaying a lot of energy and wittiness. The way he played both the roles of a street thief and a royal prince with ease is captivating. The very beautiful Naomi Scott brought elegance and royalty to the role of Princess Jasmine.

The story revolves around a street thief, Aladdin, who accidentally encounters the disguised princess and further takes you through a magical roller-coaster ride as they explore each other's world. The movie emphasizes the true values of life through the characters of Aladdin, who tries to present himself as someone who he is not, and the princess, who learns that the title of a prince is not as important as the true prince at heart, who can love her dearly.

In conclusion, the movie is worth every penny that you pay for it. I highly recommend that you go right away with your parents, cousins, and friends, and have a magical experience. Kudos to Guy Ritchie and his team! The team did an extraordinary job on this movie, which is definitely going to be a blockbuster.
